# Show, do not narrate

The screen shows the relation; the text closes the precision. Not the reverse.

In the video this is literal: a shot that displays a paragraph while the voice
reads it is a slide deck, and the video gate rejects it. The visual carries the
change; the narration says what the change means.

In the document: if a paragraph describes a spatial or temporal relation and
there is no visual beside it, the visual is missing.
