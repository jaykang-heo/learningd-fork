# Reconstruction over recognition

The product question is not "did the learner follow it?" but "can the learner
derive it again with the answer hidden?"

Every run ends with a reconstruction review against the nine outcomes in spec
2.2. Recognition - "yes, that looked reasonable" - is not evidence.

Concretely: if the breakthrough is only ever presented as a finished fact, the
learner can recognize it and cannot rebuild it. Present the obstacle first, let
the representation change answer it.
